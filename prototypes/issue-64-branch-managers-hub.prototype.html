The Branch Managers hub.

The question. What does the Branch Managers hub look like, and can you tell it apart from the Dashboard at a glance? The hub holds a branch's people, its MOU records, its Events and a read-only look at the branch. The Dashboard holds what one person owes. Both start from the same access requests, so this is where the two could blur.

Flip the three layouts with the bar at the bottom or the ← → keys. Switch the seat to see the Operations Manager and Finance Manager answer the same requests from their Dashboard. Choose Side by side to put the hub next to that person's Dashboard. Amber boxes are open questions, not answers.
